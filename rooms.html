Three exchanges.
One wallet. No custody.

SeqDEX trades against the Sequentia browser extension wallet: your keys stay in the extension, every order and every settlement is signed on your side. Each exchange below is its own room with its own book and its own settlement rules. Pick by how you want to settle.

Trading needs the Sequentia wallet extension. Get it from the downloads page, install it in a Chromium browser, then connect. Your keys never leave the extension.
